World Cup 2026 Forecast

Jun 2026 - now

A from-scratch statistical model of the 2026 World Cup. A Dixon-Coles bivariate-Poisson scoreline model (maximum-likelihood fit, 3-year time decay) with an Elo cross-check and a connectivity-weighted squad-value prior, feeding a Monte Carlo simulator that plays the full 48-team tournament over the real draw and host advantage.
